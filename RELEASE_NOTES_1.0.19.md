Endless Sky Accessible 1.0.19, for Endless Sky 0.11.4 on Windows 10 and 11.

Unofficial screen reader accessibility mod. Not made or endorsed by the Endless Sky developers. Developed with AI assistance (Claude, by Anthropic).

Requirements: Endless Sky 0.11.4 (free), from Steam or the official GitHub download (EndlessSky-win64-v0.11.4.zip), and a screen reader (NVDA or JAWS) or Windows speech.

Install: download EndlessSky_Access_1.0.19.zip below, extract it, run Install.bat, then start "Endless Sky Accessible" from the desktop shortcut. Start with docs/GETTING_STARTED.txt.

Highlights:
- Every screen spoken, with F2 describing the screen and reading its keys one at a time.
- Map: Left and Right choose mission destinations and nearby systems, Enter plans the route, Up and Down give trade prices.
- Hints after take-off and arrival say what to do next; the game pauses on arrival (Control+P toggles).
- Shipyard and outfitter: Space reads values one at a time with comparison, A reads all.
- Warnings for jumps without a route and for low fuel; ship chatter can be muted (Control+F9).

If the game hangs while loading or crashes after a few minutes on a laptop with integrated graphics, see docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md (the "Reduce large graphics" preference usually fixes it).

The full list of changes is in docs/CHANGELOG.md. Source code: the accessibility branch of this repository.
