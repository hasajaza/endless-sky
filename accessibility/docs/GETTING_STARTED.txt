Endless Sky Accessible - Getting started
========================================

This guide explains the basic loop of the game: take a job, fly to another
star system, land, get paid. The keys below are the game's default keys.


1. What the game is about
-------------------------

You own a small spaceship and must pay off its loan. You earn money by
carrying passengers and cargo for jobs, by trading goods, and later by
fighting pirates. Every trip follows the same steps:

  on a planet:  take a job, buy goods, then depart
  in space:     open the map, plan a route, jump
  arrived:      land on the planet the job asks for

You do not have to steer the ship yourself for any of this. The game's
autopilot flies the ship when you press J (jump) or L (land).


2. On a planet
--------------

When you land you hear "Landed on" and a list of places. Up and Down arrows
move through them, Enter opens one. The letters work too:

  J  Job Board: jobs you can take. Up and Down choose a job, A accepts it.
     A job says where to go and what it pays.
  T  Trading: buy goods cheaply here and sell them where the price is high.
     Plus buys one ton, Minus sells one ton.
  P  Spaceport: news and special story missions.
  S  Shipyard, O Outfitter: buy ships and equipment.
  I  Player Info: your money, ships and missions.
  D  Depart: take off into space.

Escape on a planet opens the main menu (E on the main menu returns to the game).


3. Planning a route on the map
------------------------------

After taking off, you hear what to do next, for example
"No route planned. Press M for the map..."

  M          open the map. It says where you are and lists your mission
             destinations.
  Right      the first choice is your mission destination, for example
             "Mission Passenger to New Greenland: Arcturus, 1 jump away".
             Right and Left go through the other systems you can reach,
             nearest first.
  Enter      plans the route to the system you chose. It says how many jumps
             and how much fuel the route needs.
  M again    closes the map.

Systems you have never visited may be called "unexplored system": the game
does not show their names until you go there or buy a map of the area at an
outfitter.


4. Jumping
----------

  Take off first (D on the planet). On a planet, J opens the job board.

  J  jump. The autopilot turns the ship and jumps to the next system of the
     route. You hear "Entering hyperspace" and then "Entering the ... system".

If the route has several jumps, press J again in each system. After every
arrival you hear the next step, for example "Next jump: Sol. Press J to jump."


5. Landing
----------

When the system you arrive in has your mission's planet, you hear
"Your mission destination New Greenland is in this system. Press L to land."

  L  land. The autopilot flies to the planet and lands. Press L again before
     landing to choose another planet of the system.

When you land on the right planet, the job is finished and you are paid.


6. Finding out where you are
----------------------------

These work at any time while flying:

  Control+D  destination: your route, the next jump and what to do next
  Control+L  location: the system you are in
  Control+S  status: shields, hull, fuel, speed
  Control+O  speed and heading (where you move and where you face)
  Control+N  nearby planets and where they are
  Control+M  your missions
  Control+R  repeat the last announcement

On any screen, F2 describes the screen; press F2 again to hear its keys one
at a time, and Shift+F2 to go back.


Only press J when there is a route. Without one, the game jumps to whatever
system your ship faces, and you hear a warning; press an arrow key to cancel.

While you turn with Left or Right you hear the compass direction, and the
exact heading when you let go. You also hear "Full speed" and "Stopped".
Shift+Down stops the ship. Control+Shift+O turns these announcements off.

7. Fuel
-------

Each jump uses fuel (usually 100). You can refuel on most inhabited planets,
which happens automatically when you land. If the map says
"not enough fuel", land somewhere to refuel first.

If you run out of fuel in an empty system: press Shift+Down to stop, press
Control+F to hear friendly ships, select one with N, press T to hail it,
then H to ask for help. It flies over and gives you fuel.


8. More than one ship
---------------------

When you buy a second ship, you only fly one: your flagship. The others are
escorts: they follow you and fight by themselves, and they jump and land with
you. You give them orders:

  G  gather around me     H  hold position here     F  attack my target
  Y  hold fire on or off  Z  pick up flotsam        Shift+G  next formation

Orders go to all escorts, or only to the selected ones. Escort groups, for
example group 1 fighters and group 2 heavy ships:

  1. Shift+N targets your next escort (and selects it alone).
  2. Control+Shift+N adds it to the selection. Repeat Shift+N and
     Control+Shift+N for each ship you want.
  3. Control+1 saves the selection as group 1.

Later, press 1 to select group 1, then give an order (G, H, F...). Shift+2
adds group 2 to the selection. Shift+N after your last escort clears the
selection, so orders go to the whole fleet again. Control+F lists your
ships with their condition and formation. J with escorts: hold J until they
are ready, then let go so the whole fleet jumps together.

To fly another ship as your flagship: land, press I (player info), select
the ship with Up and Down, and move it to the top of the list with
Control+Shift+Up. The first ship in the list is your flagship. Escorts need their own crew and fuel.

9. Your first trip
------------------

The game starts with a story: an old captain named James asks for a ride to
New Greenland, one jump away. To do it:

  1. On New Boston, press T for trading, buy Medical goods (he suggests it),
     then Escape and D to depart.
  2. Press M, then Right: "Mission ... Arcturus". Press Enter, then M.
  3. Press J and wait for "Entering the Arcturus system".
  4. When you hear "Your mission destination New Greenland is in this
     system", press L.
  5. On New Greenland, press T and sell your Medical goods (Minus or E).
